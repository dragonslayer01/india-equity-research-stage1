Red Flags to Screen Out Losers
  • CAC Rising YoY: Shows market saturation or competitive pressure — Swiggy, Ola Electric both show rising CAC
  • Take Rate Declining: Indicates competitive pricing pressure or GMV mix shift — watch Paytm's blended take rate
  • Contribution Margin Negative Despite Scale: Structural issue, not just timing — Swiggy Q-comm, Ola Electric
  • Cash Runway < 18 Months: Dilution risk — Ola Electric at 14 months, Swiggy at 18 months
  • Cohort Degradation: If Year-3 retention is falling vs Year-2 cohorts — structural demand issue
  • Revenue Recognition Risk: GOV vs net revenue confusion, rebates reclassification — read footnotes
  • Founder Selling at Lock-up Expiry: Honasa promoters sold ₹1,655Cr at IPO; Nykaa promoter stake decline
  • Regulatory Tail Risk: BNPL regulations (Paytm history), RBI digital lending norms

Stage 3 Scorecard — Key Learnings
  • Only 5 out of 25 new-age companies have truly attractive risk-reward: IndiaMART, Ixigo, PB Fintech, CarTrade, Naukri
  • The "profitability theatre" trap: Several companies report EBITDA profit while burning FCF via working capital — always check FCF, not EBITDA alone
  • Blinkit is the most important sub-segment to watch in Indian new-age — its EBITDA inflection will drive Eternal's re-rating
  • PB Fintech renewal cohorts are the healthiest LTV:CAC story in India (8.5x) — institutional investors still underweight
  • Ixigo's 5-year 55% revenue CAGR at P/E 55x = cheapest quality growth in the entire new-age universe
  • Swiggy is a structural avoid: Q-comm dark-store gap vs Blinkit (418 vs 1,024 stores) cannot be closed without dilutive capex
