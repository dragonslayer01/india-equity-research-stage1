🏦 Banking Sector — Deep Dive Overview

Sector Thesis (Oct 2026)

Late Cycle Correction + Rate-Easing Pivot — Nifty Bank has corrected ~15% from its 52W high. The RBI's cumulative 100bps cut cycle (Repo: 6.50% → 5.50% since Feb 2026) is the most significant macro tailwind since 2016. Transmission is beginning — MCLR-linked portfolios repricing lower, EBLR loans repricing immediately. This creates a classic NIM compression → credit growth acceleration sequence.

The sector is at an inflection: asset quality has peaked its improvement cycle (GNPA system-wide ~2.6%) and credit growth is re-accelerating from a trough. Valuations are at multi-year lows (system P/B ~1.4x vs 5Y avg ~2.0x), pricing in excessive pessimism.

Investment Horizon: 12–24 months. Key triggers: RBI rate cut pace, credit growth data, Q3FY27 NIM prints, MSME/retail stress data.

Key Risks to Banking Thesis

Macro Risks:

  • Global risk-off / USD strength → FII outflows from financials
  • Inflation spike forcing RBI to pause cuts (geopolitical oil shock)
  • Slower GDP growth → credit demand collapse below 10%
  • Rupee depreciation → imported inflation → policy reversal

  • IndusInd contagion — derivative losses disclosure was ₹1,979Cr; undiscovered books?
  • MSME/microfinance stress cycle not fully priced (Bandhan, IndusInd exposure)

🚀 New-Age / Digital Sector — Deep Dive Overview

Sector Thesis (Oct 2026)

The Great Indian Profitability Inflection — After the 2022–23 funding winter and 2024–25 correction, India's new-age digital sector is at a defining juncture. ~60% of listed new-age companies have now crossed EBITDA breakeven (vs. 20% in 2023). The market is bifurcating sharply: profitable compounders vs. structural cash burners.

The key framework: Unit Economics Viability + Profitability Stage + Cash Runway determines investment merit. Companies that converted GMV/GOV growth into positive contribution margins AND are now approaching operating leverage are in a sweet spot. Companies still burning cash at scale without a clear path to profitability are value traps.

Top-line growth alone is dead. Market now rewards PAT margin expansion, FCF generation, and ROCE improvement. The era of "grow at all costs" is definitively over.

New-Age Sector — Bull / Base / Bear Scenarios

Bull Case (20% prob): India digital economy hits $500Bn by FY28 vs $250Bn today. Profitable companies see 25–30% PAT CAGR. Sector re-rates to 2x current multiples as FCF yields become visible. Eternal reaches EBITDA margin 10%, Paytm scales financial services GMV 3x.

Base Case (55% prob): Sector consolidation continues. Top 5 profitable names (IndiaMART, Naukri, CarTrade, PB Fintech, Ixigo) compound at 18–22% CAGR. Stage 4 companies (Eternal, Paytm) expand margins by 200–300bps YoY. Swiggy and Ola Electric remain structurally challenged.

Bear Case (25% prob): Global risk-off triggers multiple compression. Loss-making cos face existential funding pressure. Eternal/Swiggy quick-commerce war intensifies, destroying economics. Ola Electric loses EV market share to Bajaj/TVS. Only Stage 5 companies survive re-rating intact.

Key Risks: Regulatory risk (RBI on BNPL, SEBI on algo trading), competition from global players (Amazon Fresh, Jio Financial), talent costs resurgence, broader market PE compression amplifying high-multiple stocks' drawdown.
