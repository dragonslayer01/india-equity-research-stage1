Portfolio Construction Philosophy

This portfolio is built on 4 pillars derived from the Stage 1–3 framework:

  1. Macro Alignment: Rate-easing cycle favors Banking; India infrastructure build-out (₹11.1L Cr capex FY27 budget) favors Capital Goods; Pharma benefits from US generic stabilization + domestic healthcare spend
  2. Quality Filter: All stocks must have ROE >15%, positive FCF, management with track record. No turnarounds except where thesis is highly specific (HDFC Bank merger)
  3. Valuation Guard: No stock bought above 2x its 5-year average P/E. Reverse DCF conducted for all high-P/E names
  4. Concentration: Top 5 positions = 42% of portfolio; top 10 = 72%. Minimum position = 2%, maximum = 12%

🏦 Banking — Why 32%?
  • RBI rate-easing cycle: 100bps cut since Feb 2026; 1–2 more likely in H1FY27
  • System GNPA at 20-year low (~2.6%); credit cycle beginning new expansion
  • Valuations at 5-year lows: system P/B 1.4x vs avg 2.0x — classic contrarian entry
  • ICICI Bank compounding: ROE 18.5% at P/B 3.15x is justified; highest conviction
  • Rate easing → CD ratio relief → NIM stabilization → credit growth >15% = sequential upgrade path
💊 Pharma — Why 22%?
  • Sun Pharma US specialty business (Ilumya, Cequa, Winlevi) = 8% CAGR; OPM 31% is peer-leading
  • India domestic pharma growing 10% YoY; chronic disease penetration rising
  • US FDA generic approvals accelerating post-COVID backlog clearance
  • Cipla respiratory + HIV portfolio = durable franchise; OPM 25% room to expand
  • Divi's CDMO: peptide synthesis + GLP-1 drug intermediates = AI drug discovery tailwind
  • Defensive sector: Pharma outperforms in market corrections — adds portfolio stability
🏗️ Infra — Why 18%?
  • Government capex: ₹11.1L Cr budgeted FY27 (2.9% of GDP) — L&T order book ₹5.8L Cr at record
  • Power sector capex: 50GW renewable target → Tata Power, CESC beneficiaries
  • Data center boom: Cummins diesel/gas gensets for DG backup → Cummins India
  • EV + housing + transmission investment → KEI Industries cables demand
  • Private capex beginning to recover (FY27E private capex +18%) → cycle extending
💻 IT — Why Only 8% (Underweight)?
  • IT sector facing twin headwinds: BFSI discretionary spend slowdown in US/Europe + AI reducing headcount growth
  • INR appreciation risk: Rupee at 83/$ — appreciation to 80 would hurt IT margins by ~100bps per 1% move
  • Valuations not cheap: Infosys at P/E 26x, TCS at P/E 28x — not enough margin of safety
  • HCL Tech chosen because: strongest BFSI exposure recovery; product mix (Mode 2/3) = less commoditized; dividend yield 3.5%
  • Coforge: mid-cap with 28% EPS CAGR guidance; BFS + insurance vertical = rate-cycle beneficiary
🛒 FMCG — Why Only 5% (Defensive Anchor)?
  • Rural demand recovering with good monsoon + rate cuts boosting rural disposable income — but priced in for most names
  • ITC: Hotels business re-rated; cigarette free cash flow machine; P/E 26x cheap; dividend yield 3.8% provides floor
  • Varun Beverages: PepsiCo franchisee in India; 40%+ volume CAGR in Africa expansion; underrated international story
  • HUL, Nestlé at premium multiples (40–60x P/E) with 8–10% growth = not value; excluded
